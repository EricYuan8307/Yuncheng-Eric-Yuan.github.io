Yuncheng (Eric) Yuan

I am an MSCA TeraWireless PhD Researcher at the University of Piraeus Research Center (UPRC), Greece.

My research focuses on rule-based machine learning for fast channel acquisition and prediction in ultra-massive MIMO THz systems.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence & Engineering Systems from Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e), where I worked on machine learning and deep learning for error correcting codes under the supervision of Prof. Alex Alvarado.
